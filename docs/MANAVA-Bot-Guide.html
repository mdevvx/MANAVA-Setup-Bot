This guide describes what each milestone delivers, every command and who can run it, and the main user flows. Companion technical docs live in the repository's docs/ folder (command-reference.md, schema.md, deployment.md, recovery.md, manava-gateway-integration.md, logging.md).

1.  Overview

The bot adds four capabilities on top of the existing MANAVA Multiverse server:

  • Squads — members form esports teams. Each squad gets its own role and four channels (member text/voice, officer text/voice), created automatically and placed in the ⚔️〔SQUADS 01〕…07 categories.
  • Unified XP economy — one Personal XP balance per member, earned from Discord chat activity and from MANAVA gameplay events. It has a Lifetime counter (never resets) and a Season counter (reset when a new season starts). A member's XP also flows 1:1 into their current squad's XP.
  • Leaderboards — a global squad leaderboard (ranked by Season XP, top 32 badged) and a per-squad member leaderboard, both shown on demand.
  • Applications — Developer and Creator/Streamer application forms with a review workflow (Approve / Reject / Request More Info) in a private staff channel.

Seasons are started and reset manually by staff. MANAVA account status ("Verified Player") and gameplay events come from the MANAVA Gateway; until MANAVA supplies credentials the bot runs in a stub mode with manual admin overrides.

2.  Milestone 1 — Squad system

2.1   What it delivers

  • Gated squad creation. A member can create a squad only if they: hold the Member role, have ≥ 1500 Personal Lifetime XP, are a Verified Player (linked MANAVA account with a confirmed email), are not already in or leading another active squad, and chose a valid, unused name (3–32 characters; letters, numbers, spaces, hyphens, underscores). Every failed check is reported back in one plain-language message.
  • Automatic Discord objects. On creation the bot makes one squad-specific role and four channels — Member Text, Member Voice, Officer Text, Officer Voice — inside the first SQUADS 0N category with room.
  • Capacity limits. 10 squads (40 channels) per category, 7 categories → a ceiling of 70 active squads. Assignment is purely by free space, never by rank. Creating past the ceiling fails with a "server at capacity, contact staff" message.
  • Roles & leadership. Every member holds the squad role + the shared Squad Member rank role; the leader also holds Squad Leader; officers hold Squad Officer (max 2 per squad).
  • Channel visibility. Member channels are visible to that squad's members and to staff (Admin, MANAVA Team, Senior Moderator, Moderator). Officer channels are visible only to that squad's Leader + Officers and staff.
  • Cross-squad isolation. Officer-channel access is granted per individual member, not via the shared "Squad Officer" role, so an officer of one squad can never see another squad's officer channels.
  • Safe destructive actions. Remove, Demote, Transfer Leadership and Disband all require an explicit Confirm / Cancel step — nothing fires on the first click. A squad can never be left without a Leader.
  • Disband & 30-day archive. On disband, access is revoked immediately and the squad drops off the leaderboard, but the channels stay (hidden) for 30 days before a daily job deletes them. The squad record, its XP history and audit trail are kept permanently, and the name stays reserved forever. Within the 30-day window a squad can be fully restored (/admin restore-squad).

2.2   Flows

Create a squad

  1. Member runs /squad create name:<name>.
  2. The bot checks every eligibility gate. If any fail, it lists them and stops — nothing is created.
  3. The squad role and four channels are created in the first category with space; the creator is made Leader.
  4. The creator gets a confirmation (and a note if their DMs are closed, since join requests arrive by DM).

Apply and join

  1. Member runs /squad apply squad_name:<name> (name auto-completes from active squads).
  2. The bot checks the applicant isn't already in a squad and that it can DM them, then records the request and DMs a receipt.
  3. The squad's Leader and Officers each get a DM with Approve / Reject buttons.
  4. A reviewer clicks a button. On Approve the bot re-checks the one-squad rule and the level's member cap, then assigns the squad role + Squad Member and grants channel access.
  5. The applicant is DMed the decision. The buttons keep working even after a bot restart.

Manage members

  1. Invite — a Leader/Officer runs /squad invite user:@member; the same one-squad and capacity checks apply, then the member is added directly.
  2. Promote / Demote — the Leader runs /squad promote / /squad demote. Promote is capped at 2 officers; Demote asks to confirm.
  3. Remove — a Leader/Officer runs /squad remove user:@member [reason], confirms, and the member loses roles and access. The Leader can't be removed.
  4. Leave — any member runs /squad leave (no cooldown; they can join another squad immediately). A Leader must transfer or disband first.

Transfer leadership & disband

  1. Transfer — the Leader runs /squad transfer-leadership user:@member and confirms. Leadership moves; the outgoing Leader becomes an Officer if there's room, otherwise a Member.
  2. Disband — the Leader runs /squad disband [reason] and confirms. Access and rank roles are removed at once, the squad leaves the leaderboard, and a 30-day archive timer starts.
  3. Restore (staff) — within 30 days, /admin restore-squad squad_name:<name> recreates the role, re-activates the squad, re-adds the members it had, and restores access. Members who have since joined another squad are skipped; if the former Leader has, the restore is refused until that's resolved.

3.  Milestone 2 — XP economy, leaderboards & MANAVA integration

3.1   Unified Personal XP

  • One balance per member, fed by two sources: Discord chat and MANAVA gameplay events. Every gain increases both the Lifetime counter (never reset — this is what the 1500 squad-creation gate reads) and the Season counter (reset by "Start New Season").
  • Discord chat XP: +15 XP for a message of at least 10 characters, with a 60-second per-member cooldown. Only counts in normal channels — an admin-managed exclusion list removes staff / log / bot / applications channels (individual channels or whole categories). A single message can never grant XP twice.
  • Voice XP is intentionally not implemented (out of scope).

3.2   Squad XP & member capacity

  • Each squad has Lifetime Squad XP (permanent) and Season Squad XP (seasonal). When a current member earns +X Personal XP, +X is added to both squad counters and to that member's contribution figure.
  • XP earned before joining is never back-filled; XP already contributed stays with the squad after the member leaves.
  • A squad's level (1–7) is derived from its Lifetime Squad XP against configurable thresholds, and the level sets the member cap:

Thresholds are editable with /xpconfig set-threshold; the member caps are fixed by specification.

3.3   MANAVA gameplay events

MANAVA's Gateway pushes one signed webhook per event to the bot. Three event types are consumed: match completed, tournament registered, and tournament placement (which also carries the finishing place and whether the player won a paid prize slot). Each event grants a configurable amount of XP.

Every event is processed in a fixed order, and a repeated event is a guaranteed no-op:

  1. Verify the request signature (or reject it).
  2. Validate the payload shape.
  3. Check the event id against the processed-events log — if already seen, stop and grant nothing.
  4. Resolve the MANAVA player to a Discord member. If unknown, record the event and grant nothing.
  5. Compute the XP for the event type and apply it to Personal Lifetime + Season XP, and to Squad XP if the member is in a squad.
  6. Mark the event id processed and write a log entry.

A test harness — /xpconfig simulate-manava-event — runs a synthetic event through this exact pipeline so the whole flow can be exercised before MANAVA's side is live.

3.4   XP configuration

XP amounts and level thresholds are stored in the database, not in code, and are changed live with /xpconfig set-xp / set-threshold — no redeploy. Defaults:

3.5   Leaderboards

  • Global squad leaderboard — active squads ranked by Season Squad XP, with the top 32 badged. No automatic qualification or bracket sync (out of scope).
  • Per-squad member leaderboard — rank, member, Personal Lifetime XP, Personal Season XP, and contribution to the squad.
  • Both are on-demand slash commands and use a single efficient query each.

4.  Milestone 3 — Applications, seasons, account linking, reliability

4.1   Applications

Two application types, both reviewed in a private staff channel (#applications-review by default; per-type channels can be set with /admin set-application-channel).

TypeFields collectedOn approval
Developer
/apply developer		 Company / Studio · Project / Game · Website / Portfolio URL · Integration Interest (dropdown) · Relevant Experience · Contact Email · optional Additional Comments. Discord id, username and timestamp are captured automatically. Removes Developer Pending, assigns Developer.
Creator / Streamer
/apply creator-streamer		 Profile Type (Creator / Streamer / Both) · Primary Platform · Region · Audience Size (dropdowns) · Profile / Channel URL · Content Languages · Average Views / Live Viewers · Content Examples · Contact Email · optional Additional Comments. Assigns Creator and/or Streamer per the Profile Type. No pending role for this type.
  • The form is a 3-step flow — dropdowns, then a modal for the text fields, then an optional comments step. Contact email and URLs are format-checked on submit; a bad value shows a Fix & resubmit button that reopens the form pre-filled.
  • One open application per type per member. After a rejection, the same type can be resubmitted only after 30 days (enforced by the bot).
  • The review channel is locked to Admin and MANAVA Team; Moderator and Senior Moderator are explicitly excluded.
  • Application contact details are never written to general logs — they stay inside the review channel and the applicant's own DMs.

Flow — submit and review

  1. Member runs /apply developer (or /apply creator-streamer), fills the form.
  2. The bot assigns Developer Pending (Developer only), posts a summary card to the review channel, and DMs the applicant a receipt.
  3. A reviewer clicks a button on the card:
    • Approve → assigns the role(s), removes Pending, DMs the applicant.
    • Reject → optional reason, removes Pending, DMs the applicant, starts the 30-day clock.
    • Request More Info → the reviewer types a question; the applicant is DMed a Respond button.
  4. If more info was requested, the applicant clicks Respond, types an answer; it is attached to the same application and the card returns to the reviewers.
  5. The reviewer confirmation names the applicant with a mention. All buttons survive a bot restart.

4.2   Seasons

  • Staff-only. /season start opens a season; /season end freezes Season XP; /season status shows the current one.
  • /season new (with a confirm step) ends the current season and resets every member's Personal Season XP and every squad's Season Squad XP to 0. Lifetime XP and per-member contribution are never touched. The action and the staff member who ran it are recorded.

4.3   Account linking / Verified Player

Linking a Discord account to a MANAVA account happens on the MANAVA website — there is no bot command for it. The bot only reads the result.

  • Gateway mode (once MANAVA supplies credentials): when a member tries to create a squad, the bot asks the MANAVA Gateway whether that Discord id is linked and email-verified, and shows the right guidance — "link your MANAVA account" vs "confirm your email" — with a link to the MANAVA site.
  • Stub mode (default until then): staff set the flag manually with /admin set-verified, and map a MANAVA id with /admin link-manava-account. These commands stay available as a support tool even after Gateway mode is on.

Flow — a member becomes eligible

  1. Member runs /squad create and is told they aren't a Verified Player, with a link to the MANAVA site.
  2. Member signs in / registers on the MANAVA site with Discord and confirms their email.
  3. Member runs /squad create again — the bot re-checks with the Gateway, sees they're linked and verified, and (assuming the other gates pass) creates the squad.

4.4   Reliability

  • Duplicate / retried MANAVA events never grant XP twice (checked before any XP is computed).
  • Bad or unsigned webhooks are rejected; a database blip returns a retryable response so the Gateway re-delivers; a temporary Gateway outage falls back to the last known value.
  • All persistent buttons (squad join requests, application review, applicant "Respond") keep working across a bot restart.
  • The bot never grants Admin, MANAVA Team, Senior Moderator or Moderator — a hard deny-list blocks it even on a logic bug.
  • Squad creation rolls back its Discord objects if the database write fails — no half-made squads.
  • Every administrative change (XP corrections, verification, config edits, season actions, application decisions, squad lifecycle) is written to an audit log with the actor, timestamp, and before/after values.
  • If a member has DMs closed, /squad apply tells them to enable DMs before continuing, and a reviewer is told when a decision DM couldn't be delivered.

7.  Server prerequisites & operating notes

7.1   Must already exist on the server

  • Roles (matched by name): Admin, MANAVA Team, Senior Moderator, Moderator, Squad Leader, Squad Officer, Squad Member, Member.
  • Categories (created by staff, exact names): ⚔️〔SQUADS 01〕 through ⚔️〔SQUADS 07〕.
  • The bot's own role must sit above the squad and application roles it assigns.

7.2   Created automatically by the bot

  • Roles Developer Pending, Developer, Creator, Streamer, and the #applications-review channel (locked to Admin + MANAVA Team; Moderator / Senior Moderator denied). Created on startup or !sync if missing.
  • Per-squad roles and channels, on each squad creation.

8.  MANAVA Gateway integration

The bot and the MANAVA Gateway have exactly two contact points.

8.1   Bot → Gateway: "is this Discord account a verified player?"

When a member hits the squad-creation gate, the bot asks the Gateway about their Discord id and gets back whether the account is linked to a MANAVA account, the MANAVA player id, and whether the email is confirmed. The bot caches this so later gameplay events can be attributed to the right member.

8.2   Gateway → Bot: gameplay events

The Gateway sends one signed webhook per event (match completed, tournament registered, tournament placement) to a URL MANAVA registers on their side. The bot verifies the signature, de-duplicates by event id, and grants the configured XP. If the bot can't be reached or replies with an error, the Gateway retries on its own schedule.

Two modes

Gateway mode is active once MANAVA_GATEWAY_BASE_URL, DISCORD_BACKEND_API_KEY and WEBHOOK_SIGNING_SECRET are set. Until then the bot is in stub mode: /admin set-verified and /admin link-manava-account stand in for the identity lookup, and the event pipeline is exercised with /xpconfig simulate-manava-event. Switching modes is an environment-variable change and a restart — no code change.

Full request/response detail is in docs/manava-gateway-integration.md.

9.  Known limitations & items owned by MANAVA

9.1   By design

  • The 60-second text-XP cooldown is kept in memory, so it resets on a bot restart (at most one extra grant right after a redeploy).
  • Text-XP grants are best-effort during a database outage — they're logged and skipped, not queued.
  • A season reset is recorded but final standings aren't snapshotted — export the leaderboard before /season new if a record is wanted.
  • A member's "contribution" figure is per-membership-stint lifetime, not per-season.
  • Disbanded squads keep hidden channels for 30 days before deletion; recover with /admin restore-squad within that window.
  • Run only one instance of the bot per token.
  • A gameplay event for a MANAVA player the bot has never looked up is recorded but grants no XP until an identity lookup warms the cache (confirmed acceptable for launch).

9.2   Provided / done by MANAVA

  • Real DISCORD_BACKEND_API_KEY and WEBHOOK_SIGNING_SECRET values.
  • Registering this deployment's event webhook URL with the Gateway.
  • A value for the prize-slot XP bonus (currently 0, so it has no effect).
  • The production MANAVA account-linking URL.
  • Deployment and CI/CD (GitLab → VPS). The repository provides the container, a runtime contract (docs/deployment.md) and the database migrations.
